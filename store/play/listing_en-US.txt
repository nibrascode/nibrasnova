Nibras Nova: Halal Browser

Family-safe browser: adult sites blocked, halal image blur, prayer times

== Full description (2023/4000) ==
Nibras Nova is a fast, privacy-minded Android browser built for Muslim families. Safety features run on your device, and your browsing is not sent to us.

FAMILY-SAFE BY DEFAULT
• Adult (18+) sites are always blocked, in normal and private tabs, using an on-device list.
• SafeSearch is enforced on Google, Bing and DuckDuckGo, and YouTube Restricted Mode is applied.
• Halal search mode: images on search results and web pages are checked by an on-device model, and images not judged safe are shown blurred. Images are never uploaded. Tap a blurred image to show it (PIN-protected when parental controls are on). Automatic detection can make mistakes.

PRAYER TIMES & QIBLA
• Prayer times on the home screen with a live countdown and the Hijri date.
• Umm al-Qura (Makkah) method by default; other methods can be chosen.
• Choose your country, then your city (works offline), or use your location.
• Notifications 10 minutes before each prayer and when the time begins, configurable per prayer.
• Qibla compass with direction in degrees and distance to the Kaaba.

PARENTAL CONTROLS
• A parent PIN (4–6 digits, stored only on the device) protects settings, history clearing and safety options.
• Add extra blocked sites and set a daily time limit.
• Family profiles: separate Adult and Child profiles with their own history, bookmarks and tabs. The Child profile opens only parent-approved sites (nibrascode.com and Quran sites by default). Returning to Adult needs the PIN.

FOR LEARNERS & DEVELOPERS
• HTML playground: write HTML, CSS and JavaScript, run it in a live preview and see console output. Templates, saved snippets and .html export.
• Developer console, desktop mode and reader mode.
• "Explore" section with articles from nibrascode.com.

MORE
• 5 languages: Azerbaijani, English, Turkish, Russian and Arabic (with right-to-left layout).
• Private tabs, ad and tracker blocking, bookmarks and history with JSON backup.
